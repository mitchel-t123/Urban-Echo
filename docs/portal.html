Welcome Email — Stage 2
Subject: Welcome to the Urban Echo Crew! 🎉  ·  Send: After compliance & HR complete
URBANECHO
WELCOME TO THE
URBAN ECHO CREW.

Hey [First Name]! 👋

We're so excited to welcome you to the Urban Echo team. After reviewing your application, we'd love to have you on board as part of our crew. We work with some incredible schools across Perth and we can't wait to have you alongside us.

This email covers everything you need to know before your first event, what happens next, what we expect from our crew, and a link to the full handbook. Give it a read when you get a chance; it won't take long.

What Happens Next
1
Join the WhatsApp Crew GroupWe'll add you to our crew WhatsApp group, this is where jobs get posted, updates go out, and the team stays connected. Keep notifications on!
2
Sign Your Contractor AgreementWe'll send you a short contractor agreement to sign before your first job. This covers your role, pay, and how we work together. Takes 5 minutes.
3
Confirm Your Compliance ChecksWorking with Children Check (WWC) and a National Police Clearance are mandatory for all crew. If you don't have them yet, we can point you to where to apply, they're straightforward to get.
4
Read the Employee HandbookThe handbook covers everything from what to wear to how we handle emergencies. It's a short read and everything you need to know is in there. Link at the bottom of this email.
5
Complete a Site InductionBefore going solo, Mitchel will walk you through gear, setup, and what a typical event looks like. Usually takes about an hour at a school or our storage space.
6
Shadow Your First EventYour first job will be a shadow, you'll come along, watch the show run, and learn the ropes without the pressure. After that you're ready to go.
What We Expect From You
🛡️
Child Safety — Always FirstAll our events are in schools. Child safety is non-negotiable. Always maintain professional boundaries, never be alone with students, and report anything concerning immediately.
🤝
Code of ConductRepresent Urban Echo with pride. Be respectful, punctual, and switched on. Treat everyone, students, staff, and clients, with professionalism and warmth.
⚠️
Work Health & SafetyYou're responsible for your own safety and those around you. Report hazards, use gear safely, and let Mitchel know immediately if something's not right on the job.
📱
Social MediaNever post photos of students or school events without explicit permission. Our reputation with schools depends on discretion, when in doubt, don't post.
🔒
Privacy & ConfidentialitySchool contacts, client details, and job information are confidential. Don't share venue codes, contact names, or school details outside the crew group.
🚨
EmergenciesKnow the school's evacuation plan before every show. In an emergency: stop the show, call 000 if needed, follow school staff instructions, and contact Mitchel straight after.
Good to Know

What to wear: All black, smart casual, for every event. Collared shirt preferred for formal evenings. We want to look professional and blend into the background.

Availability: Jobs come through on WhatsApp, reply ASAP so we can confirm crew. If you can't make it, give as much notice as possible. We respect that life happens, just keep us in the loop.

Pay: We pay per event, direct to your bank account. If you're a sole trader/ABN contractor you'll invoice us after the job, we'll share the details when you're onboarded.

Questions? Reach out any time. Mitchel is always happy to chat, call, text, or WhatsApp.

We'll be in touch shortly with next steps. In the meantime, feel free to reach out if you have any questions at all.

Crew Onboarding Guide
Phase 1, Review the Application
⚡ Automated: When someone submits an application, ClickUp auto-creates a task with their details, a pre-filled welcome email comment, and a 10-step onboarding checklist.
1
Open the ClickUp task
Go to the Applications list in ClickUp. The new task is named "Application, [Full Name]". Check roles, experience, availability, WWC/police status, and suburb.
2
Decide: Approve or decline
Make the call and move the task to Approved or Declined in ClickUp. If declining, add a brief note in comments for your records.
Phase 2, Welcome & Admin
3
Send welcome email + contractor agreement
Open the ClickUp task → comments, the welcome email is pre-drafted. Click Copy Email on the template page, paste into Gmail, and attach the contractor agreement before sending. Subject: Welcome to Urban Echo, You're In! 🎉
4
Confirm compliance checks
Both WWC and National Police Certificate must be on file before any event.

If not yet obtained:
• WWC: workingwithchildren.wa.gov.au, free for volunteers, ~$11 for paid workers
• Police Check: police.wa.gov.au (~$42)
5
Check they're in Slack
Confirm they've joined via the invite link in the welcome email and can see messages in #urban-echo-team-chat. Say hello and introduce them to the group.
6
Arrange a coffee catch-up
Book a quick coffee with Mitchel before their first event, 20–30 mins. Great chance to get to know them, answer questions, and make them feel genuinely welcomed.
Phase 3, Their First Event
7
Complete a site induction
Mitchel walks them through gear, setup, and a typical event. About an hour, book before their first job.
8
Shadow their first event
First job is always a shadow, they observe the full show with no pressure. Confirm date/time/venue via Slack, brief them on all-black attire and arrival time.
9
Add to the crew pool
Tick off everything below, then mark all ClickUp checklist items complete and move the task to Complete.

• Add to the crew roster and collect bank details/ABN
• Add them to Rentman (crew management)
• Create a website login for the crew portal
• Add to payroll
• Post a welcome in #urban-echo-team-chat
⚠️ Never send someone to an event solo until they have completed the site induction, a shadow event, and both compliance checks are confirmed on file.
