Hey [First Name]! 👋

We're so excited to welcome you to the Urban Echo team. After reviewing your application, we'd love to have you on board as part of our crew. We work with some incredible schools across Perth and we can't wait to have you alongside us.

This email covers everything you need to know before your first event, what happens next, what we expect from our crew, and a link to the full handbook. Give it a read when you get a chance; it won't take long.

What Happens Next
1
Join Our SlackAll crew comms happen on Slack: shift updates, event briefs, and team chat. Check your inbox for the invite link and join the workspace. Keep notifications on!
2
Sign Your Contractor AgreementWe'll send you a short contractor agreement to sign before your first job. This covers your role, pay, and how we work together. Takes 5 minutes.
3
Confirm Your Compliance ChecksWorking with Children Check (WWC) and a National Police Clearance are mandatory for all crew. If you don't have them yet, we can point you to where to apply, they're straightforward to get.
4
Read the Employee HandbookThe handbook covers everything from what to wear to how we handle emergencies. It's a short read and everything you need to know is in there. Link at the bottom of this email.
5
Complete a Site InductionBefore going solo, Mitchel will walk you through gear, setup, and what a typical event looks like. Usually takes about an hour at a school or our storage space.
6
Shadow Your First EventYour first job will be a shadow, you'll come along, watch the show run, and learn the ropes without the pressure. After that you're ready to go.
What We Expect From You
🛡️
Child Safety: Always FirstAll our events are in schools. Child safety is non-negotiable. Always maintain professional boundaries, never be alone with students, and report anything concerning immediately.
🤝
Code of ConductRepresent Urban Echo with pride. Be respectful, punctual, and switched on. Treat everyone, students, staff, and clients, with professionalism and warmth.
⚠️
Work Health & SafetyYou're responsible for your own safety and those around you. Report hazards, use gear safely, and let Mitchel know immediately if something's not right on the job.
📱
Social MediaNever post photos of students or school events without explicit permission. Our reputation with schools depends on discretion, when in doubt, don't post.
🔒
Privacy & ConfidentialitySchool contacts, client details, and job information are confidential. Don't share venue codes, contact names, or school details outside the crew group.
🚨
EmergenciesKnow the school's evacuation plan before every show. In an emergency: stop the show, call 000 if needed, follow school staff instructions, and contact Mitchel straight after.
Good to Know

What to wear: All black, smart casual, for every event. Collared shirt preferred for formal evenings. We want to look professional and blend into the background.

Availability: Jobs come through on Slack, reply ASAP so we can confirm crew. If you can't make it, give as much notice as possible. We respect that life happens, just keep us in the loop.

Pay: We pay per event, direct to your bank account. If you're a sole trader/ABN contractor you'll invoice us after the job, we'll share the details when you're onboarded.

Questions? Reach out any time on Slack, or call or text Mitchel directly.

Work Health & Safety Policy

Urban Echo is committed to providing a safe working environment for all staff, contractors and volunteers. This policy applies to all events, venues and work activities.

9.1 · Workplace Health & Safety

Urban Echo is committed to safe working practices. Urban Echo employees and contractors are expected to:

  • Follow Safe Work Australia guidelines
  • Comply with venue WHS requirements
  • Use safe lifting and manual handling practices
  • Report hazards immediately
  • Keep cables safely secured to reduce trip hazards

9.2 · Electrical & Equipment Safety

Urban Echo employees and contractors are expected to:

  • Operate equipment within their level of competency
  • Report damaged equipment immediately
  • Never bypass electrical safety protections
  • Transport and store equipment safely
  • Use tagged and tested equipment where required

9.3 · Drug & Alcohol Policy

Urban Echo employees and contractors must not:

  • Attend shifts under the influence of drugs or alcohol
  • Consume alcohol during shifts unless specifically approved
  • Engage in unsafe behaviour caused by impairment

Breaches may result in removal from site and termination of engagement.

9.4 · Incident Reporting

Any injuries, hazards, near misses, equipment damage, or safety concerns should be reported to management as soon as possible. Use the Incident Response Form or Risk Assessment Form on the portal.

9.5 · Ladders & Working at Height

  • Never use a ladder alone. Always have a spotter present
  • Inspect ladders before use and do not use damaged equipment
  • Keep three points of contact when on a ladder
  • Do not overreach. Reposition the ladder instead

9.6 · Emergency Procedures

  • Familiarise yourself with evacuation routes and assembly points at each venue
  • In an emergency, follow venue staff instructions and call 000 if required
  • Know the location of first aid kits at every event
  • Do not re-enter a building during an evacuation until the all-clear is given
Urban Echo Pty Ltd · July 2026

Code of Conduct & Child Safety Policy

This policy applies to all Urban Echo staff, contractors and volunteers. It sets out the standards of behaviour expected at all times when representing Urban Echo.

1 · Professional Conduct

  • Represent Urban Echo with pride, professionalism and warmth at all times.
  • Be punctual. Arrive on time and ready for your role. Contact your supervisor immediately if you are running late.
  • Dress appropriately for the event. Smart casual or as directed by your supervisor.
  • Be respectful to clients, venue staff, students and fellow crew members at all times.
  • Avoid discriminatory, offensive or harassing language or behaviour.
  • Use of personal devices during events should be minimal and discreet.

2 · Child Safety

  • A valid Working With Children Check (WWCC) must be held before working at any school event.
  • Never be alone with a student. Maintain appropriate professional boundaries at all times.
  • Photography and recording of students is strictly prohibited unless explicitly authorised in writing.
  • Do not give students personal contact details, follow them on social media, or communicate with them outside of work channels.
  • Any child safety concern must be reported to your supervisor immediately and documented.
  • Comply with all school visitor requirements including sign-in procedures and lanyard/badge protocols.

3 · Health & Safety

  • Follow all WHS procedures and site-specific safety requirements.
  • Report hazards, near-misses and incidents via the portal forms immediately.
  • Use correct manual handling techniques. Ask for assistance with heavy loads.
  • Only operate electrical equipment you are trained and authorised to use.
  • Use ladders and elevated equipment safely and never alone.
  • Know the location of first aid kits and emergency exits at each venue.

4 · Drugs, Alcohol & Smoking

  • Zero tolerance for drugs or alcohol while working or on-call. Do not arrive impaired.
  • Smoking and vaping is prohibited at all school venues and during any Urban Echo event.
  • Any breach of this policy may result in immediate stand-down and termination.

5 · Confidentiality & Social Media

  • Do not share client, event or pricing information outside the team.
  • Do not post photos or videos from events on personal social media without prior approval.
  • Do not make public statements on behalf of Urban Echo.
  • Treat all internal communications and documents as confidential.

6 · Communication & Availability

  • Respond to booking requests and team messages promptly via Slack.
  • Notify your supervisor as early as possible if you are unable to make a confirmed shift.
  • Keep your availability updated in Rentman.
  • Attend briefings when required and confirm all event details before the day.

To acknowledge and sign this policy, download the form from the Code of Conduct: Sign & Return card in Onboarding Resources and SOPs.
