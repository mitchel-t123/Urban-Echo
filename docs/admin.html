Crew Onboarding Guide
Phase 1 — Review the Application
⚡ Automated: When someone submits an application, ClickUp auto-creates a task with their details, a pre-filled welcome email comment, and a 10-step onboarding checklist.
1
Open the ClickUp task
Go to the Applications list in ClickUp. The new task is named "Application — [Full Name]". All their details are in the custom fields: roles, experience, availability, WWC/police status, suburb.
2
Decide: Approve or decline
Review and make the call. Move the task to Approved or Declined in ClickUp. If declining, add a brief note in the comments for your records.
Phase 2 — Welcome & Admin
3
Send the welcome email + contractor agreement
Open the task → scroll to comments — the welcome email is pre-drafted with their first name. Open the Welcome Email template page, click Copy Email, paste into Gmail. Attach the contractor agreement to the same email before sending — covers their role, pay, ABN/payment details, and confidentiality. Subject: Welcome to Urban Echo — You're In! 🎉
4
Confirm compliance checks
Both WWC and National Police Certificate must be on file before any event — no exceptions. Ask them to forward confirmation once received.

If they don't have them yet:
• WWC: workingwithchildren.wa.gov.au — free for volunteers, ~$11 for paid workers
• Police Check: police.wa.gov.au or via an ACIC-accredited provider (~$42)
5
Check they're in Slack
Confirm they've joined via the invite link in the welcome email. Make sure they're in #urban-echo-team-chat and can see messages. Say hello and introduce them to the group.
6
Arrange a coffee catch-up
Book a quick coffee with Mitchel before their first event — 20–30 mins, somewhere local. Great chance to get to know them, answer any questions, and make them feel genuinely welcomed to the team.
Phase 3 — Their First Event
7
Complete a site induction
Mitchel walks them through gear, setup, and a typical event. About an hour — book before their first job.
8
Shadow their first event
First job is always a shadow — they observe the full show with no pressure. Confirm date/time/venue via Slack, brief them on all-black attire and arrival time.
9
Add to the crew pool
Tick off everything below, then mark all ClickUp checklist items complete and move the task to Complete.

• Add to the crew roster and collect bank details/ABN
• Add them to Rentman (crew management)
• Create a website login for the crew portal
• Add to payroll
• Post a welcome in #urban-echo-team-chat
⚠️ Never send someone to an event solo until they have completed the site induction, a shadow event, and both compliance checks are confirmed on file.

All crew comms happen on Slack — shift updates, event briefs, and team chat. Click below to join the workspace:

Step 2 — Sign the contractor agreement

We've attached a contractor agreement to this email. Please read through and sign it — takes about 2 minutes. You can sign digitally on our website or scan and return it.

Step 3 — Compliance checks

Before your first event we'll need your Working With Children Check and National Police Certificate on file. If you don't have these yet, no stress — just get them sorted before your first gig and send us a copy.

What to expect next

We'll be in touch via Slack to arrange a quick coffee catch-up with Mitchel before your first event. From there, your first shift will be a shadow — you come along, watch the show, and get a feel for how we run things. No pressure, just vibes.

After that you'll be added to the crew pool and we'll start rostering you in for events that suit your availability.

Good to know
  • Events are posted in Slack — keep an eye on #urban-echo-team-chat
  • Dress code is always all black unless told otherwise
  • Arrive 15–30 mins before the crew call time
  • Any questions? Just message us in Slack — we're always around
