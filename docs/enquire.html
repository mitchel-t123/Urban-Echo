How it works

Sound sorted in three steps.

No complicated contracts. No hidden fees. Just reliable sound and AV from people who know what they're doing.

1

Tell us about your event

Fill in the enquiry form below or give us a call. Tell us what kind of event, when, where, and roughly how many people. We'll get back to you within one business day.

2

We send you a quote

We'll put together a clear, itemised quote based on your event needs. No surprises. If you need equipment hire, extra operators or extended hours, it's all in there.

3

We show up and deliver

On the day, our technician arrives early, sets up, and operates the sound so everything runs without a hitch. You focus on your event. We focus on making it sound great.
